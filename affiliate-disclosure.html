How Affiliate Relationships Influence Our Content

Affiliate status never influences our editorial recommendations.

Our editorial process is independent of our business relationships. Specifically:

  • We do not accept payment in exchange for positive coverage.

  • If we cannot recommend a product with a high commission, we say so honestly.

  • Most affiliate links are visually distinguished with a "View on Amazon →" button or similar call-to-action styling.

MouseTestHQ is reader-supported. Affiliate commissions, combined with display advertising (Google AdSense), allow us to:

We deeply appreciate your support — but only purchase through our links if the product genuinely suits your needs. We never want anyone to feel pressured to click.

Despite these connections, our opinions are our own. We are committed to honest, accurate content.
