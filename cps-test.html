What Is a Good CPS Score?

Clicks per second (CPS) is the standard measure of mouse click speed. Average users hit 6–7 CPS with a regular click; gamers using techniques like jitter clicking reach 10–14 CPS, and the very best Kohi/Jitterclick records exceed 20 CPS. Anything over 10 CPS is excellent for most use cases.

CPS Benchmarks (5-second test)

  • Beginner: 4–6 CPS
  • Average: 6–8 CPS
  • Good: 8–10 CPS
  • Excellent: 10–14 CPS
  • Elite (Jitter/Butterfly): 14+ CPS

Tips to Improve Your CPS

  • Relax your hand — tension slows you down and causes fatigue.
  • Use your wrist rather than your whole arm for short bursts.
  • Try jitter clicking — vibrate your fingers so the button bounces between clicks. Works best on lightweight mice with low-resistance switches.
  • Try butterfly clicking — use two fingers alternating on the same button. Requires a double-clicking-friendly mouse.
  • Lower your mouse DPI — at very high DPI you waste motion on micro-adjustments.

How This Test Works

Each click event is timestamped using performance.now() for millisecond precision. We count clicks only on the test stage, ignoring right-clicks, keyboard input, and touch events outside the stage. Your best score is saved to your browser's localStorage — nothing is sent to a server.

Privacy Note

All counting happens in your browser. We don't see, store, or transmit your click data. The "best score" is stored locally on your device only.
