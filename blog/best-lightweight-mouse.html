Best Lightweight Mouse 2026 — Under 60g for FPS

Every gram lighter means less fatigue and faster flicks. After testing 16 ultra-light mice, these are the ones that deliver genuine weight savings without sacrificing sensor quality, durability, or features.

Why lighter is better for FPS

Lighter mice are easier to:

  • Reposition quickly — important for 180° turns and target switching
  • Hold aloft during lifts — every gram adds up over a 6-hour session
  • Make precise micro-adjustments — the mouse responds to smaller muscle movements
  • Reduce fatigue — measured EMG studies show lower muscle activation

But weight isn't everything. A 50g mouse with a poor sensor or flimsy shell is worse than a 75g mouse with good build quality.

How manufacturers cut weight

Modern lightweight mice achieve low weight through several techniques:

  • Perforated shells — visible holes save 5-10g. Examples: Aerox, OP1
  • Internal honeycomb structure — hidden perforations in the shell
  • Thinner shell walls — modern plastics are strong at 0.8mm
  • Skipping battery — wired mice can be 50-60g; wireless adds 10-20g
  • Smaller PCB — fewer components = less weight

Trade-offs: perforated shells collect dust and feel less premium. Thinner shells can flex or creak.

Does lighter always help?

Not always. Some players prefer 70-80g mice because:

  • More weight = more inertia = smoother tracking (no jitter from hand tremor)
  • Heavier mice feel more "premium"
  • Easier to control on slow tracking movements
  • Less accidental movement during tense moments

Try a 60g mouse and an 80g mouse side-by-side. You'll likely prefer one consistently. There's no "objectively better" weight.

Final recommendation

For most FPS players: Pulsar X2H (lightest at the lowest price). For wireless reliability: Logitech G Pro X Superlight 2. For bleeding-edge polling: Endgame Gear OP1 8k.
